[ Safety Culture Maturity Matrix ]

LVL 01

Pathological Culture

Safety is not important. We only do the bare minimum to avoid getting caught.

Management doesn't know and doesn't want to know.

LVL 02

Reactive Culture

We react only when an accident occurs or during an inspection.

Management knows, but shows no initiative.

LVL 03

Compliance Culture

Regulatory compliance is important. We have risk management systems in place.

Management cares, but doesn't always know how to act.

LVL 04

Proactive Culture

Continuous improvement is key. Everyone is involved in OHS.

Management cares and knows how to improve the system.

LVL 05

Mature Culture

OHS is our priority. It is built into our DNA and business development.

Management monitors, measures, and genuinely leads.

Probability of accidents: High Low

Maturity climbs left to right. Accident probability falls the other way — every level you gain removes a class of incident, not just a line in a report.
