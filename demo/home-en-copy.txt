=== SAFETY CULTURE MATURITY MATRIX (5 stages, axis: Probability of accidents High -> Low) ===
1. Pathological Culture — Safety is not important. We only do the bare minimum to avoid getting caught. | Management doesn't know and doesn't want to know.
2. Reactive Culture — We react only when an accident occurs or during an inspection. | Management knows, but shows no initiative.
3. Compliance Culture — Regulatory compliance is important. We have risk management systems in place. | Management cares, but doesn't always know how to act.
4. Proactive Culture — Continuous improvement is key. Everyone is involved in OHS. | Management cares and knows how to improve the system.
5. Mature Culture — OHS is our priority. It is built into our DNA and business development. | Management monitors, measures, and genuinely leads.
