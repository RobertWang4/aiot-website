# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
- Procurement and tender reviewers at industrial plants (manufacturing, logistics, labs) in Poland and abroad, plus plant/EHS managers evaluating an outsourcing partner. They open the site from a bid document or an email link, on desktop, in a hurry, comparing several vendors.
- Job to be done: within 30 seconds confirm "this company is credible, modern, and covers what we need", then drill into one service area or find contact details.
- Emotion: confidence and calm authority. No hype, no hard sell.

### Brand Personality
- AIoT P.S.A. — three words: **modern, intelligent, leading**.
- Voice: precise, quiet, assured. Short sentences. Facts over adjectives. Credentials (since 2008, ISO, PCA accreditation) speak for themselves.
- Brand marks: navy #0B1B4A and accent blue #2F6BFF from the logo. Logo is a placeholder jpg for now.

### Aesthetic Direction
- White-dominant, large-scale, generous whitespace. Edge-to-edge photography of real industrial settings. Big, confident sans-serif headlines. Little copy on landing pages; depth lives on subpages.
- Reference: xchanneltech.com (white ground, simple nav, full-width photo bands, few words, dark footer). Ambition level: Apple/Siemens corporate scale rather than a small-business template.
- The "intelligent / leading" note should come from precision and restraint (grid discipline, numeric labels, crisp hairlines, refined type), not from glow effects or sci-fi motifs.
- Anti-references (explicitly rejected): dark navy-scrim hero pages, serif italic accents, marquees/ken-burns/parallax, "AI landing page" card grids with gradients, heavy use of blue, dense text.
- Light mode only. Typography: Satoshi (display) + Instrument Sans (body) + IBM Plex Mono (numerals/labels, sparingly). Blue is an accent (CTA, hover, numerals, one headline word), never a surface. Full detail in DESIGN.md.

### Design Principles
1. **Scale over ornament.** Size, whitespace and photography carry the impression; decoration does not.
2. **One idea per screen.** Each viewport-height section says one thing; detail moves to a subpage.
3. **Precision signals intelligence.** Consistent grid, numeric labels (01–10), aligned hairlines, tight typographic rhythm.
4. **Blue is a signal, not a paint.** Accents only (CTA, hover, numerals); never a background.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-reachable navigation, motion reduced to fades and respects prefers-reduced-motion.
